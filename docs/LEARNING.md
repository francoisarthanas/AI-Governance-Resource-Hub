### [Ethics of AI](<https://ethics-of-ai.mooc.fi/>)

**Publisher:** University of Helsinki  
**Format:** Self-paced course with readings and exercises · **Access:** Free; sign-in for progress, assignments and completion certificate  
**For:** Beginner; governance, policy and business professionals

**Why it matters:** A rigorous, accessible foundation in accountability, transparency, human rights and fairness, with exercises rather than a glossary alone.

**Put it into practice:** Start with Accountability and AI Ethics in Practice; use the exercises to identify affected people and challenge an AI deployment rationale.

<a id="aigp-training"></a>

### [IAPP Artificial Intelligence Governance Professional (AIGP) Training](<https://iapp.org/train/aigp-training>)

**Publisher:** IAPP  
**Format:** Self-paced or instructor-led professional training · **Access:** Paid; MyIAPP account required; exam is a separate purchase  
**For:** Beginner–intermediate; professionals establishing a governance knowledge baseline

**Why it matters:** Structured coverage of the AI lifecycle, impacts, applicable laws and risk management from the certification body itself.

**Put it into practice:** Use the curriculum to identify knowledge gaps before selecting training; pair with actual risk-assessment and review work.

<a id="fairness-video"></a>

### [21 fairness definitions and their politics](<https://fairmlbook.org/tutorial2.html>)

**Publisher:** Arvind Narayanan / FAccT  
**Format:** Recorded tutorial · **Access:** Free  
**For:** Intermediate; governance reviewers and technical partners

**Why it matters:** Explains why a single fairness score cannot settle a decision involving competing values and trade-offs.

**Put it into practice:** Watch before approving a fairness metric; document whose interests the metric captures and what it misses.

<a id="evals-video"></a>

### [Why AI Evals Are the Hottest New Skill for Product Builders](<https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BsWxPI9UM4c>)

**Publisher:** Lenny's Podcast  
**Format:** Video / podcast interview · **Access:** Free YouTube video; newsletter page/transcript marked paid  
**For:** Beginner–intermediate; governance reviewers, product owners and assurance teams

**Why it matters:** An accessible demonstration of reviewing real traces, discovering failures and turning evaluation criteria into explicit requirements.

**Put it into practice:** Start at 16:51 for error analysis; ask what evidence would justify accepting or rejecting an AI release.

<a id="procurement-podcast"></a>

### [AI procurement with Dr. Cari Miller](<https://www.enz.ai/ai-governance-podcast-ai-procurement-with-dr-cari-miller>)

**Publisher:** Enzai AI Governance Podcast  
**Format:** Vendor-produced podcast / video episode · **Access:** Free; platform accounts may be needed for some listening features  
**For:** Beginner–intermediate; procurement, third-party risk and governance teams

**Why it matters:** Connects AI governance to vendor due diligence, unmanaged entry points and the evidence needed to challenge supplier claims.

**Put it into practice:** Turn the discussion into vendor questions about intended use, testing, documentation and ownership.

<a id="verification-podcast"></a>

### [A Deep Dive Into IVOs with Fathom's Bri Treece](<https://www.csis.org/podcasts/ai-policy-podcast/deep-dive-ivos-fathoms-bri-treece>)

**Publisher:** CSIS — The AI Policy Podcast  
**Format:** Podcast / video interview · **Access:** Free  
**For:** Intermediate; assurance, policy and governance professionals

**Why it matters:** Examines independent verification as a mechanism for checking AI developers' claims and discusses relevant regulatory analogies.

**Put it into practice:** Start at 5:32 for the proposed Independent Verification Organization model; assess reviewer independence, evidence access and accountability.

### [Introduction to the NIST AI RMF 1.0: An Explainer Video](<https://www.nist.gov/video/introduction-nist-ai-risk-management-framework-ai-rmf-10-explainer-video>)

**Publisher:** NIST  
**Format:** Video · **Access:** Free  
**For:** Beginners and anyone onboarding colleagues to AI governance

**Why it matters:** A direct introduction from the framework's publisher makes a useful common starting point for a governance discussion.

**Put it into practice:** Watch before reading the framework, then name one decision your team must make under each of its four functions.
