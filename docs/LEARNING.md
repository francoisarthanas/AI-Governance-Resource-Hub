### [Ethics of AI](<https://ethics-of-ai.mooc.fi/>)

**Publisher:** University of Helsinki  
**Format:** Self-paced course with readings and exercises · **Access:** Free; sign-in for progress, assignments and completion certificate  
**For:** Beginner; governance, policy and business professionals

**Why it earns a place:** A rigorous, accessible foundation in accountability, transparency, human rights and fairness, with exercises rather than a glossary alone.

**Use it to:** Start with Accountability and AI Ethics in Practice; use the exercises to identify affected people and challenge an AI deployment rationale.

**Version / status:** Living course; reviewed 2026-09-12  
**Reviewed:** 2026-09-12

### [IAPP Artificial Intelligence Governance Professional (AIGP) Training](<https://iapp.org/train/aigp-training>)

**Publisher:** IAPP  
**Format:** Self-paced or instructor-led professional training · **Access:** Paid; MyIAPP account required; exam is a separate purchase  
**For:** Beginner–intermediate; professionals establishing a governance knowledge baseline

**Why it earns a place:** Structured coverage of the AI lifecycle, impacts, applicable laws and risk management from the certification body itself.

**Use it to:** Use the curriculum to identify knowledge gaps before selecting training; pair with actual risk-assessment and review work.

**Version / status:** Current provider offering; reviewed 2026-09-12  
**Reviewed:** 2026-09-12

<summary>Source review notes</summary>

</details>

### [21 fairness definitions and their politics](<https://fairmlbook.org/tutorial2.html>)

**Publisher:** Arvind Narayanan / FAccT  
**Format:** Recorded tutorial · **Access:** Free  
**For:** Intermediate; governance reviewers and technical partners

**Why it earns a place:** Explains why a single fairness score cannot settle a decision involving competing values and trade-offs.

**Use it to:** Watch before approving a fairness metric; document whose interests the metric captures and what it misses.

</details>
