<a id="imda-agentic-framework"></a>

### [Model AI Governance Framework for Agentic AI](<https://www.imda.gov.sg/-/media/imda/files/about/emerging-tech-and-research/artificial-intelligence/mgf-for-agentic-ai.pdf>)

**Publisher:** IMDA, Singapore  
**Format:** Governance framework with enterprise case studies · **Access:** Free  
**For:** GRC, cybersecurity, enterprise owners

**Why it matters:** A practical enterprise foundation for agent permissions, human accountability, control selection, testing and monitoring, illustrated with deployment cases.

**Put it into practice:** Turn its four dimensions into an agent review checklist and identify the evidence needed before deployment.

<a id="governing-agentic-systems"></a>

### [Practices for Governing Agentic AI Systems](<https://openai.com/index/practices-for-governing-agentic-ai-systems/>)

**Publisher:** OpenAI; Shavit, Agarwal, Brundage et al.  
**Format:** Research white paper · **Access:** Free  
**For:** GRC, risk owners, policy and research

**Why it matters:** A foundational treatment of suitability, constrained actions, approvals, activity visibility, monitoring, attribution and interruptibility.

**Put it into practice:** Define who can stop an agent, which actions need approval and what records make responsibility traceable.

<a id="agent-visibility"></a>

### [Visibility into AI Agents](<https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.13138>)

**Publisher:** Alan Chan et al.; ACM FAccT 2024  
**Format:** Research paper · **Access:** Free  
**For:** Governance reviewers, audit, research

**Why it matters:** Connects agent identifiers, monitoring and activity records to accountability, while examining privacy and power trade-offs.

**Put it into practice:** Design an evidence and logging policy that explains which agents acted, for whom and under what oversight.

<a id="intelligent-delegation"></a>

### [Intelligent AI Delegation](<https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.11865>)

**Publisher:** Nenad Tomašev, Matija Franklin and Simon Osindero  
**Format:** Research preprint · **Access:** Free  
**For:** Governance designers, architects, researchers

**Why it matters:** Proposes a framework for delegating tasks, authority and responsibility across humans and agents, with explicit boundaries and accountability.

**Put it into practice:** Review agent handoffs: delegated task, allowed scope, evidence of capability, escalation and responsibility for failure.

<a id="ethics-ai-assistants"></a>

### [The Ethics of Advanced AI Assistants](<https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.16244>)

**Publisher:** Iason Gabriel et al.; Google DeepMind researchers  
**Format:** Research paper · **Access:** Free  
**For:** Governance, policy, product and research

**Why it matters:** Extends agent reviews beyond cybersecurity to manipulation, user trust, privacy, autonomy, access and societal impacts.

**Put it into practice:** Select the chapters relevant to your use case when preparing impact assessments or human-agent interaction requirements.

<a id="agent-evals"></a>

### [Demystifying Evals for AI Agents](<https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/demystifying-evals-for-ai-agents>)

**Publisher:** Anthropic  
**Format:** Practical engineering article · **Access:** Free  
**For:** Governance reviewers, evaluation teams, developers

**Why it matters:** Explains tasks, trials, graders, traces and real outcomes, plus why repeated agent runs and human calibration matter.

**Put it into practice:** Assess whether evaluations measure successful authorized action, distinguish capability tests from regression checks, and add checks for the governance requirements of your use case.

<a id="evals-faq"></a>

### [AI Evals: Everything You Need to Know](<https://hamel.dev/blog/posts/evals-faq/>)

**Publisher:** Hamel Husain and Shreya Shankar  
**Format:** Practical reference / FAQ · **Access:** Free  
**For:** Intermediate; reviewers working with AI development teams

**Why it matters:** Answers concrete questions about error analysis, human labels, LLM judges, guardrails and evaluating agent workflows.

**Put it into practice:** Start with 'How do I evaluate agentic workflows?' and 'What's the difference between guardrails & evaluators?'; translate the answers into evidence requests.
