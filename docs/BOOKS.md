### [AI Snake Oil](<https://www.normaltech.ai/p/starting-reading-the-ai-snake-oil>)

**Publisher:** Arvind Narayanan and Sayash Kapoor / Princeton University Press  
**Format:** Book; free introductory chapter and companion exercises · **Access:** Paid book; free first chapter and selected companion material  
**For:** Beginner–intermediate; all governance practitioners

**Why it earns a place:** Builds disciplined skepticism about AI capability claims and distinguishes risks across predictive and generative applications.

**Use it to:** Read the introduction first; use its questions to challenge a vendor's validation evidence and suitability claims.

**Version / status:** Original 2024; creator page confirms updated preface and epilogue for 2025 paperback  
**Reviewed:** 2026-09-12

### [The Alignment Problem: Machine Learning and Human Values](<https://brianchristian.org/the-alignment-problem/>)

**Publisher:** Brian Christian / W. W. Norton  
**Format:** Book · **Access:** Paid; may be available through public libraries  
**For:** Beginner–intermediate; practitioners who need conceptual depth

**Why it earns a place:** Makes specification failures, bias and mismatches between human intent and machine behavior understandable through research history and cases.

**Use it to:** Use it to sharpen questions about objectives, proxy measures and unintended consequences during design and deployment reviews.

**Version / status:** 2020; foundational book predating the current LLM-agent ecosystem  
**Reviewed:** 2026-09-12

<summary>Source review notes</summary>

### [Fairness and Machine Learning: Limitations and Opportunities](<https://fairmlbook.org/>)

**Publisher:** Solon Barocas, Moritz Hardt and Arvind Narayanan / MIT Press  
**Format:** Open textbook · **Access:** Free full online text and PDF; paid print edition  
**For:** Intermediate–advanced; governance and technical assurance teams

**Why it earns a place:** Explains the limits of fairness metrics, the legitimacy of automated decisions, discrimination testing and data practices.

**Use it to:** Start with chapters 2, 7 and 9; use the discussion prompts when reviewing decisions that affect people's opportunities.

**Version / status:** 2023 publication; online page last updated 2023-11-10  
**Reviewed:** 2026-09-12

<details>
<summary>Source review notes</summary>

Opened official authors' book site with full-PDF link, chapter descriptions and publication details. Mathematical chapters require more technical background; legal discussion is U.S.-focused and must be checked against current law.

</details>
