### [AI Snake Oil](<https://www.normaltech.ai/p/starting-reading-the-ai-snake-oil>)

**Publisher:** Arvind Narayanan and Sayash Kapoor / Princeton University Press  
**Format:** Book; free introductory chapter and companion exercises · **Access:** Paid book; free first chapter and selected companion material  
**For:** Beginner–intermediate; all governance practitioners

**Why it matters:** Builds disciplined skepticism about AI capability claims and distinguishes risks across predictive and generative applications.

**Put it into practice:** Read the introduction first; use its questions to challenge a vendor's validation evidence and suitability claims.

<a id="alignment-problem"></a>

### [The Alignment Problem: Machine Learning and Human Values](<https://brianchristian.org/the-alignment-problem/>)

**Publisher:** Brian Christian / W. W. Norton  
**Format:** Book · **Access:** Paid; may be available through public libraries  
**For:** Beginner–intermediate; practitioners who need conceptual depth

**Why it matters:** Makes specification failures, bias and mismatches between human intent and machine behavior understandable through research history and cases.

**Put it into practice:** Use it to sharpen questions about objectives, proxy measures and unintended consequences during design and deployment reviews.

<a id="fairness-book"></a>

### [Fairness and Machine Learning: Limitations and Opportunities](<https://fairmlbook.org/>)

**Publisher:** Solon Barocas, Moritz Hardt and Arvind Narayanan / MIT Press  
**Format:** Online textbook · **Access:** Free full online text and PDF (CC BY-NC-ND 4.0); paid print edition  
**For:** Intermediate–advanced; governance and technical assurance teams

**Why it matters:** Explains the limits of fairness metrics, the legitimacy of automated decisions, discrimination testing and data practices.

**Put it into practice:** Start with chapters 2, 7 and 9; use the discussion prompts when reviewing decisions that affect people's opportunities. Its legal discussion focuses on the United States.
