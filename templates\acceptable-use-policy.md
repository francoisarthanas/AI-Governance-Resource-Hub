# Acceptable use of AI — policy template

> Adapt this template to your organization, workforce arrangements, systems, and legal obligations. Pair rules with approved tools and a fast exception path.

## Purpose and scope

This policy governs employees, contractors, and other authorized users who procure, build, configure, connect, or use AI for organizational work. It covers public and enterprise AI services, embedded AI, models, copilots, agents, and automated decision systems.

## Required behavior

Users must:

1. use approved accounts, systems, and integrations for organizational information;
2. classify information before entry and follow privacy, security, records, confidentiality, IP, and contractual rules;
3. remain accountable for outputs and actions; verify material facts, calculations, citations, code, and decisions;
4. disclose AI assistance where policy, law, professional standards, or reasonable user expectations require it;
5. provide meaningful human review for consequential decisions and preserve appeal/escalation paths;
6. report harmful, anomalous, insecure, discriminatory, privacy-invasive, or unauthorized behavior promptly;
7. complete intake and approval before a new high-impact use, external integration, production agent, or material expansion.

## Prohibited behavior

Unless explicitly approved, users must not:

- enter secrets, credentials, regulated/sensitive personal data, confidential client information, or restricted IP into unapproved services;
- use AI as the sole decision-maker for employment, credit, insurance, healthcare, education, housing, legal, safety, or similarly consequential outcomes;
- represent generated material as verified, fabricate sources/evidence, impersonate a person, or create deceptive media;
- bypass access controls, monitoring, content safeguards, human approvals, spending limits, or other technical/policy controls;
- connect agents to email, code execution, financial transactions, production administration, or sensitive repositories without approved least-privilege controls;
- use unlicensed data/content, conduct unlawful surveillance or profiling, or discriminate;
- deploy externally facing AI or materially change an approved system without change review.

## Accountability and enforcement

The AI governance owner maintains the approved-tools list, intake channel, training, exceptions, and review cadence. Security/privacy/legal/HR/records teams retain their existing authority. Exceptions must be documented, time-bounded, approved by named risk owners, and monitored. Violations may result in access removal and action under applicable policy or contract.
