# AI go-live checklist

Record an evidence link, owner, and date for every checked item. “Not applicable” requires a rationale and approver.

## Accountability and scope

- [ ] Named business, product, model/system, risk, privacy, security, and operations owners.
- [ ] Intended users, affected people, uses, prohibited uses, jurisdictions, and deployment boundary are current.
- [ ] Inventory, risk tier, impact assessment, system card, decision record, and exceptions are approved.
- [ ] Applicable legal, regulatory, contractual, sector, and internal requirements are mapped.

## Data, model, and system

- [ ] Data provenance, authority/consent, quality, representativeness, minimization, retention, and deletion are verified.
- [ ] Model, prompt, retrieval, tools, agents, memory, dependencies, permissions, and configuration are versioned.
- [ ] Security/privacy threat model and high-risk remediation are complete.
- [ ] Supplier evidence, contract controls, change notices, support, and exit arrangements are acceptable.
- [ ] User notice, limitations, instructions, accessibility, consent/choice, and appeal/escalation paths are ready.

## Evaluation

- [ ] Evaluation covers representative tasks, relevant slices, misuse, robustness, privacy, security, safety, and human factors.
- [ ] Predefined release thresholds are met; test independence and reproducibility are proportionate to impact.
- [ ] Agentic deployments pass injection, tool misuse, delegation, identity, limit, recovery, and approval-bypass scenarios.
- [ ] Known limitations, residual risks, compensating controls, and time-bounded exceptions are accepted by named owners.

## Operations and incident readiness

- [ ] Production metrics, thresholds, owners, dashboards, alerts, sampling, and review cadence are configured.
- [ ] Logs capture versions, inputs/outputs as permitted, actions, tools, approvals, overrides, outcomes, and trace IDs.
- [ ] Canary scope, rate/spend/action limits, safe fallback, rollback, kill switch, and credential revocation are tested.
- [ ] Incident runbook, severity model, on-call path, legal/privacy/security escalation, evidence preservation, and exercises are complete.
- [ ] Reassessment triggers cover incidents, drift, threshold breaches, material changes, new uses, and scheduled review.
